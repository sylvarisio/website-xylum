xylum vs a traditional ERP

an ERP was built for a factory.
xylum is built for you.

One system for your work and your money, without the weight.

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning: one system for a whole company's work and money. The idea is good. The weight is the problem.

It grew up in factories and big firms. It is powerful, and it expects a team to set it up and feed it.

side by side

where xylum is different.

the question a traditional ERP xylum
Who is it built for? a traditional ERPLarge companies with many departments. xylumConsultants, freelancers and small service teams.
How do you start? a traditional ERPWith a setup project, often led by a paid partner. xylumYou sign in and switch off the apps you do not need.
What do you see first? a traditional ERPMenus, forms and a dashboard of numbers. xylumOne sentence that says what today is about.
Who does the routine work? a traditional ERPYour team, one screen at a time. xylumXylum does the chores, every night.
Who writes the reminders? a traditional ERPYou do, or a template does. xylumXylum drafts them. You read each one and send it.
Where is the AI? a traditional ERPAdded later, on top of old screens. xylumBuilt in from the start, and it asks first.
Can your own tools connect? a traditional ERPThrough connectors and custom work. xylumNearly every app has its own API and agent tools.
Does it keep the accounts? a traditional ERPYes. A full accounting ledger is built in. xylumNo. Your accountant keeps the ledger.
Does it run a factory or a warehouse? a traditional ERPYes. That is what it was made for. xylumNo. Xylum does not track stock, warehouses or production.

ERP products differ, and this is the common pattern.

to be fair

size

You have many departments and a team to run the system.

A traditional ERP is built for that.
