questions

questions, answered straight.

Short answers, in plain words. Each one says what Xylum does today.

the basics

what is xylum?

Xylum is one app that runs a small service business. It keeps your clients, quotes, projects, hours, invoices and costs in one place.

It does the routine work. What it writes for you waits for your yes.

is xylum an ERP?

Yes, in the useful sense. It is one system for your work and your money.

It is built for small service businesses, not for factories. It leaves the accounting ledger to your accountant.

how is xylum different from a traditional ERP?

A traditional ERP is built for large companies and needs a team to run it. Xylum is built for consultants, freelancers and small service teams.

It opens on an answer, not a dashboard. It does the chores every night. See Xylum vs a traditional ERP.

who is xylum for?

Xylum is for people who sell their time and their skill. That means independent consultants, freelancers, and small studios and agencies.

It is not for factories or warehouses. See who it's for.

which apps are in xylum?

For the work: projects, timesheets, calendar, todo, helpdesk, booking and a wiki.

For the money: sales quotes, invoicing, expenses, subscriptions, contracts and purchasing.

For people and records: contacts, a sales pipeline, a price list and document templates. See every app, one line each.

the help

what does xylum do on its own?

Every night it works through the chores. It closes quotes that ran out and raises your repeating charges.

It warns you before a domain, certificate or licence runs out. It moves contracts on when their dates come. When a late invoice is due a reminder, it drafts one.

can xylum send something to a client without asking me?

Not unless you tell it to. What Xylum writes for you waits as a draft until you say yes. A public booking page does email the person who booked.

The assistant cannot approve its own work. Xylum can send payment reminders alone, but only after you switch that on.

can i see what xylum did?

Yes. Changes to your records go in the audit log. It says who made the change, when, and through which door.

Xylum cannot edit or delete an entry in the log. The database refuses it.

which AI does xylum use?

The assistant runs on a model from Anthropic.

The key stays on the server. It is never kept in the app or its database.

can my own AI assistant work with xylum?

Yes. Xylum speaks MCP, the standard that AI assistants use to work with other software.

An outside assistant can read, and make edits you can undo. It cannot send or delete. We set up its access with you.

the fit

does xylum replace my accountant?

No. Xylum is not an accounting system, and it does not file your taxes.

It runs the work that comes before the accounts: quotes, hours, invoices and costs.

can i run more than one company in xylum?

Yes. Each company keeps its own records, under one sign-in.

Your personal money has its own corner too.

which languages does xylum speak?

Five: English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and French. Each person picks their own. Not every screen is translated yet.

does xylum work on a phone?

Yes. Xylum runs in the browser, in a layout made for phones.

Your phone's calendar can read your Xylum events over CalDAV.

do i have to use every app?

No. Most apps can be switched off. Contacts and the price list always stay on.

what happens to my email address?
