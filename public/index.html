every app

every app, one line each.

  • workprojectsMilestones, tasks and subtasks for a client. Hours burn against the plan.
  • worktimesheetsA running timer and manual entries. See your week against your target.
  • workcalendarEvents with repeats and all-day entries. Your phone reads them over CalDAV.
  • worktodoFast capture. Type, press Enter, and the box is ready for the next thought.
  • workhelpdeskSupport tickets and their messages. A ticket can name the client it is for.
  • workbookingAppointment types with a length, a gap and a price. Two bookings never overlap.
  • workwikiNested pages in markdown. Two people cannot overwrite each other by accident.
  • moneysalesQuotes from first draft to accepted. An accepted quote turns into an invoice in one click.
  • moneyinvoicingIssue, send, track, chase and write off. Each invoice gets its number once.
  • moneyexpensesRecord and sort costs, with the receipt attached. Company and personal costs stay apart.
  • moneysubscriptionsCosts that repeat, entered once. Domains and licences warn you before they run out.
  • moneycontractsWhat you agreed, with whom and for how long.
  • moneypurchasingPurchase orders and the bills that answer them. A supplier is a contact.
  • moneyreportsNumbers across apps.
  • people and recordscontactsOne record for each real person or company. Most other apps point to it.
  • people and recordscrmA pipeline of deals over your contacts. You name the stages and set their order.
  • people and recordscatalogYour price list. One record for each thing you sell.
  • people and recordstemplatesThe documents your clients see. HTML templates, turned into PDF.
  • people and recordslibraryThe invoices, quotes and contracts Xylum sent, kept as they went out.
  • personalfinanceCash, bank and card accounts and what moved through them.
  • personalinventoryWhat you own. A sold or scrapped item stays on record with its date.
  • platformsystemCompanies, people and access, apps on and off, email, AI settings and the audit log.
  • platformportalA door for one client contact to see their own invoices and tickets.
