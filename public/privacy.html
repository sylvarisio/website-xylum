privacy

privacy

What we keep when you ask for an invitation, who handles it, and how to have it removed.

what we keep

Your email address, your name if you gave it, the time you signed up, and the sentence you agreed to.

To stop abuse, the server remembers which internet address a request came from, for one hour. That is held in memory only and is never written down.

why we keep it

To send you one email when early access opens. Nothing else. We keep it because you asked us to, by ticking the box.

who handles it

Only we read it. We are Sylvaris, the company that makes Xylum.

Two companies work for us. Infomaniak, in Switzerland, runs this website and holds the list. Lettermint, in the European Union, carries a notice of each request to our mailbox.

They handle your details only to do that work. This site uses no tracking.

how long we keep it

Until we have written to you about early access, or until you ask us to remove it.

how to be removed

Write to privacy@sylvaris.io. We delete the whole entry. You can also ask to see what we hold about you.
